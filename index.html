HOW TO PLAY

Read the whole ice

  1. Climb twenty levels. Reach each accuracy target to unlock faster challenges, new situations, and near-impossible elite tests.
  2. Scan every option. Defenders may take away a teammate, the shot, or your skating lane.
  3. Rush open ice. Choose Rush when a clear channel leads toward the net or either corner.
  4. Reset under pressure. Choose Regroup when no teammate and no forward skating lane is open.
  5. Decide quickly. Press A, W, D, R, or S—or tap a choice—before the clock expires.
  6. Trust your eyes. Lane guides fade as you advance and disappear in the elite levels.
  7. Earn Cheese Points. Make smart reads and complete levels, then spend your points on player gear in the Locker.

Tip: moving backward to keep possession is better than forcing a play into pressure.
